# Omsi-Hub privacy statement

**Version 1, effective 16 October 2026.**

This statement explains which personal data Omsi-Hub (the app, the game and omsihub.com) processes, why, and what your rights are. The app and the website show this same text. When it changes in a way that matters, the version number goes up and you will be asked to agree again.

## Who we are

Omsi-Hub is a free, non-commercial add-on for the bus simulator openOMSI. Omsi-Hub is the controller for the data described here.

**Contact:** privacy@omsihub.com

## Playing without an account

You can play Omsi-Hub fully without an account. Your companies, careers and settings then stay on your own computer, and we receive nothing personal. Even then, a few things go over the internet:

- **Update checks.** The app asks GitHub which version is the newest, and downloads updates from there. GitHub sees your IP address, as with any website.
- **"Playing now" counter.** While you play, the game tells openOMSI's counter service that one more copy is running. It sends a random id that is new on every start, the game version and the kind of system (for example "windows"). It sends no name, no IP log and nothing else from your computer. You can switch this off in Settings → General.
- **Multiplayer.** When you share or join a company, the launchers connect directly to each other. Anyone you give your session code to can see your IP address. Nothing of this goes through our servers.
- **Phone and tablet.** The game's phone and tablet screens work only inside your own home network.

## With an account (optional)

An account adds friends, invites, the leaderboard and cloud saves. For it we store:

| Data | Why |
| --- | --- |
| Email address and password | To sign you in, verify your address and reset your password. The password is stored only as a secure hash. |
| Sign-in through Google, Discord or Steam (if you choose it) | We receive the account id and, where the provider gives it, your email address. We never receive your password there. |
| Display name and friend code | So friends can find and recognise you. |
| Friends, friend requests and blocks | For the friends list. |
| Online status, last seen, and your session code while you host | So your friends can see that you are playing and join you. Only your friends see this. |
| Invites | So you can invite a friend into your session. An invite expires after 15 minutes. |
| Career statistics (points, level, duties, minutes driven, km) | For your profile. They appear on the public leaderboard only if you switch that on. |
| Up to 2 cloud saves of companies | So you can continue on another computer. Saves stay private to your account. |
| The version of this statement you agreed to, and when | To show that you agreed. |

**Legal basis.** We process this data to provide the account features you ask for (GDPR art. 6(1)(b)). The leaderboard is based on your consent (art. 6(1)(a)), which you can withdraw at any time by switching it off.

## Where your data is kept and who processes it

| Processor | What it handles |
| --- | --- |
| **Supabase** | Accounts, the database and cloud saves. Servers in Frankfurt, Germany (EU). |
| **Our email provider** | Sends the verification and password-reset emails. |
| **Google, Discord or Valve (Steam)** | Only if you sign in through them, under their own privacy policies. |
| **The host of omsihub.com** | Serves the website. Its server logs may briefly contain IP addresses. |

We do not sell your data, show no ads, and use no tracking or advertising cookies. The website stores your sign-in session only in your own browser.

## How long we keep it

- **Your account data:** kept until you delete your account.
- **Invites:** expire after 15 minutes.
- **Deleting your account:** you can do this yourself, in the app (Account → Delete account) or on omsihub.com. It removes your profile, friends, invites, statistics and cloud saves at once.

## Your rights

You have the right to:
- access your data, and to correct it;
- have it deleted;
- receive it in a portable form;
- object to its use, or restrict it;
- withdraw your consent.

You can do most of this yourself in the app or on the website. For anything else, email privacy@omsihub.com.

You may also complain to a data protection authority. In the Netherlands that is the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens.

## Age

You need to be at least 16 to make an account, or have permission from a parent or guardian. You can play without an account at any age.

## Security

- All connections are encrypted (HTTPS).
- Each account can only reach its own data, plus what its friends may see. This is enforced by the database itself (row-level security).
- The app and website contain no secret keys.

## Changes

We will change this statement when Omsi-Hub changes what it does with data. A new version gets a new number. The app and the website then ask you to agree again before you use your account.
